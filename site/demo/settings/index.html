Controls, guardrails and what the engine is configured to do. Config lives in config/*.yaml; changes there take effect on restart.

Kill switch

Off
Stops every send immediately, from every mailbox. Replies are still read.
Also from the server terminal: node dist/cli.mjs kill on

Outreach emails

Autopilot
Autopilot: emails that pass every check send by themselves on schedule. Anything the checker flags waits on Approvals, as do the first 50.

Replies to leads

Automatic
Every reply gets a draft right away. It waits 4h for anyone to edit, send or hold it, then sends by itself. Hot leads and unclear replies always wait for a person, and the team gets an email.

Email notifications

Who gets an email when something happens, delivered to the address in "Send to". Sent as "OpenGTM" from jamie@outreach-mail.example, each with a link to the conversation.
Nobody to notify yet
People appear here once they're on the Team page. On your laptop the login skips the workspace, so this list fills in on the server.
