When emails go out

Emails land during the recipient's working day, never at night or on a day off.
DaysMonday to Friday (the recipient's day)
Hours9:00 AM to 4:30 PM in the recipient's own time zone
In team time (PT)
Eastern companies: 6:00 AM to 1:30 PM
Central companies: 7:00 AM to 2:30 PM
Mountain companies: 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM
Pacific companies: 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM
Spacing30 seconds to 5 minutes apart from each mailbox, at random
Follow-upEmail 2 on day 7 if they haven't replied
RepliesDrafted answers send themselves 4 hours after the reply arrives, unless someone edits, sends or holds them. Hot leads always wait for you.
