How it works

From a list of companies to a booked call, every weekday.

Each stage is a scheduled job that's safe to retry, so a crash or restart never sends twice.

Features

Everything outbound needs, in one place you own.

Finds the right people

Pulls companies and decision makers from a lead database, your CSV lists or company sites, and scores every one against the customers you describe.

Researches each company

Reads their own website and keeps facts with the page they came from, so every personal line can be traced to a source.

Writes honest emails

Short, plain-text, one question. It may only claim what a fact or your proof points say; a checker and an AI reviewer block the rest.

Sends from your mailboxes

Google, Microsoft or any SMTP, in each recipient's business hours, ramping up slowly with one mailbox per conversation.

Handles every reply

Labels each reply in context, stops the sequence, honors opt-outs, follows referrals, and drafts the answer.

Hands you hot leads

When someone says yes you get an email right away, a drafted reply with your booking link, and a one-page brief for the call.

Honest by construction

It can only say what it can back up.

Every claim must cite a researched fact or one of your proof points. Numbers it can't cite, spam phrases, fake familiarity, fake urgency and fake "Re:" subjects are rejected before an email goes anywhere. You approve the first 50 yourself.

Inbox

Every reply, read and handled.

Interested replies become hot leads with an answer ready. Objections get a respectful draft that sends after a review window. Opt-outs are suppressed forever.

Deliverability

Built not to burn your domains.

Guardrails run on their own every few minutes, and new mailboxes ramp up slowly.

Cost

No seats. No platform fee.

You pay only for what you use: an AI key, mailboxes, verification and lead data. Every cost is logged, and a daily cap stops paid calls.

Quickstart

Running in about five minutes.

You need Docker and Node.js 22 or newer. Nothing sends until you've added your postal address, a sender and mailboxes.

FAQ

Questions

Is cold email legal?

In the US, yes, when it's honest and easy to opt out of (CAN-SPAM). OpenGTM is US-only by design: it won't send without your real postal address, every email says who it's from and has a working unsubscribe, and opt-outs are permanent.

Which AI models does it use?

OpenAI by default, Anthropic with one config file, or a local model through any OpenAI-compatible server like Ollama. Each job (writing, checking, sorting replies) can use a different model.

Do I need my own mailboxes?

Yes. Use inboxes on domains that aren't your main one, so your real domain is never at risk. Pre-warmed inboxes from a vendor are the fastest start; upload their CSV and each login is tested.

Where do leads come from?

AI Ark's people search is built in. You can also import your own CSV lists, or find people on companies' own websites. Only use lead data whose terms allow outbound email.

Does it send without me?

After you approve the first 50 emails, emails that pass every check send on their own, inside caps and business hours. Anything the checker flags waits for you, hot leads always get a person's reply, and a kill switch stops everything at once.

Where does my data live?

In your own Postgres database, on your own server. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the services you configure: your AI provider, your mailboxes, and your verification and lead providers.
